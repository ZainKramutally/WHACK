Hook · “What if life was silicon-based?”

Earth’s crust is 28% silicon and 0.02% carbon. You are 18% carbon. Life picked carbon anyway.

Hackathon idea: let the player change how element groups behave — valence, bond strength, solvent, temperature — and watch chemistry climb into an ecosystem. This page is the research behind it, one finding per chart.

01 · The hook

Silicon outnumbers carbon 1,410 to 1 in the rock under your feet, yet your body runs the other way: 9,000 carbon atoms’ worth of mass for every silicon.

02 · Why silicon loses in water

Silicon’s chains are weak (Si–Si 222 vs C–C 346 kJ/mol) but its grip on oxygen is the strongest bond here — so in water and air, silicon ends up as rock.

05 · Our prototype

With real bond energies, our toy model already reproduces the science: carbon in water builds giant molecules, silicon in water rusts to dead Si(OH)ₙ “sand” within ~120 steps, and silicon in cold methane survives.

Toy well-mixed stochastic bonding model, N = 3,000 atoms, 3,000 steps, seed 1; “dead” = backbone atoms with ≥ 2 of 4 valences on O. Source: data/prototype-results.json, prototype/bonding_model.py, notes/07. [INFERRED] — a model, not chemistry.

06 · Science → knobs

Six player knobs, each anchored to a measured property — and the most interesting one, “oxygen hunger”, is where silicon is twice as greedy as carbon.

Carbon valueSilicon value
Prototype trap: a “stronger Si–Si” slider alone does nothing in water (scenario 5a: still 100% dead). Hydrolysis is a polarity effect, not a strength effect — the UI should say so. Tuning target from notes/07: keep Ebackbone/kT ≈ 3.5–4.5, water attack on Si ≈ 0.03× toy rate, H:X ≈ 2 to avoid gelling.

07 · How life emerges in our sim

The 3-minute demo climbs four layers — first bond at 0:05, first replicator by 0:55, first organism at 1:30, predators and three trophic levels by 2:45.

Target times with tuned presets, not measurements [INFERRED]. Source: notes/03 §6 “Witnessable milestones”. Organism promotion uses Gánti’s chemoton criteria (membrane + template + metabolism).
