## 2. Rules we should change because biology says otherwise

1. **Eye size should be hump-shaped, not monotonic.** Today `A = g19·(1.5 − light)` makes the darkest worlds have the biggest eyes. Biology says dim → bigger, but **dark with no light source → eyes regress** (cavefish, #16). Fix: cap the plastic term at about `light ≥ 0.15`. Below that, let the existing eye cost (`0.2·A²`) select eyes away, **unless glow is common nearby** (deep-sea eyes stay because bioluminescence exists). This gives a free emergent story: "in the dark, eyes vanish, until something learns to glow." It is cheap because it reuses the ledger.
2. **Give Hunger a plastic development effect: inducible armour.** Fill the "—" cell in §7's Hunger × Development: newborns get `plateCover += k·Hunger·threat` (and spine rods on Si flora under grazing). This is real (*Daphnia* helmets, transgenerational; grass silica, #9). It also solves F's own "Hunger's first effect is invisible on bodies" gap.
3. **Retire "Hunger makes horns" as science.** Horns are mostly sexual weapons (#11). Options: (a) keep horns, tag `[game]`, no slide; (b) move the *hunter* weapon to claws or gape, which F already has, and make horns/spines *prey* defence via gape-limitation (#10, sticklebacks). (b) is better science and needs no new mechanism.
4. **Couple size to oxygen, and make TSR stronger for swimmers.**
   - Add `o` to the size cap, e.g. `S_max ×(1 + k·o)`, so O-rich worlds grow giants (#5).
   - Scale the TSR amplitude by medium: aquatic or low-band swimmers get the full lerp, walkers about 1/10 (−5 vs −0.5 %/°C, #1).

   F's current TSR (2.5× length, about 15× mass across the dial) is in range for aquatic animals over ~40 °C (`[INFERRED]` 0.95⁴⁰ ≈ 0.13), but far too strong for terrestrial ones.
5. **Change R6's *explanation*, not its direction.** "Cold slows reactions → giant" is not the accepted reason. Say "cold water holds more oxygen relative to demand (debated)", or simply show the pattern and cite Atkinson/Forster.
6. **Flora glow should need oxygen too.** Fauna glow already needs `o`, so make the R8 flora glow bulb scale by `o` as well, or at least by `o > 0`. That keeps "living light burns oxygen" (#18) true everywhere in the sim. Low cost.
7. **Floating needs hollow bodies, not just dense air.** `ρ_air` up to 0.9 game units implies air approaching flesh density. Real thick air (Titan, about 5 kg/m³) never does that. Keep the mechanic, but gate float mode on high `buoy` (gas-bag volume) and say on screen "gas-filled float, like a Portuguese man o' war". Don't claim that thick air alone floats animals.
8. **Don't call stress mutagenesis biology.** It's a microbe phenomenon (#33). Keep it listed under tuning cheats only, which §10 already does. Just keep it off slides.
