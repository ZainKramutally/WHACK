# Body plans (design F): deep research

Direction: `docs/emergence/F-body-plan-first.md`, chosen in `docs/emergence/README.md`. **Chemistry is the rulebook bodies grow by. Complexity has to evolve from worms.**

Research questions:
1. **Time-to-complexity.** Can horns, legs, plates and buoyant giants evolve from a worm founder inside a 3-minute demo? What population size, mutation rate and time acceleration does that need? *(literature + headless prototype)*
2. **Biology grounding.** Which of F's chemistry → body rules have real evo-devo or ecology behind them, so they can carry `[sci]` tags and go on slides?
3. **Bodies and motion.** How do we grow and animate arbitrary morphologies in real time on three.js r187 WebGPU (lofted bodies, procedural gait, IK), within budget?
4. **Fast ecology.** How do ALife sims get producers → grazers → hunters quickly and stably?
